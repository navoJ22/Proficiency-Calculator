Usage

Use /proficiency-calculator hero:{hero name} and enter the same information as on the website. (Current Level, Target Level, Stats per 10 minutes, and more)

The bot works in servers, DMs, and group chats. You can add it to a server or install it to your apps to use it anywhere.

Note: Servers that disable "Use External Apps" permission prevent the bot from displaying results to everyone, but results will still be visible to you.

Cross Progression

Log in with Discord to share progress between the website and the bot.

The website and bot will prefill fields like Current Level, Target Level, and missions.
